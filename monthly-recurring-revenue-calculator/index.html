How this calculator works

MRR should include recurring subscription revenue using one consistent definition. One-off setup fees, services and usage spikes are normally reported separately.

MRR = active recurring customers × average monthly recurring revenue per customer.

Example

85 active customers paying an average $120 per month produce $10,200 in MRR and $122,400 annualized recurring revenue.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
