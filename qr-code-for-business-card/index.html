Why put a QR code on a business card?

A printed business card has limited space. A QR code can connect that small card to a richer online destination, such as your portfolio, service page, booking calendar, contact page or professional profile. The best destination is usually the page you want someone to visit immediately after meeting you.

How to create a business card QR code

Enter the final public URL above and generate the code. Scan-test it with your phone, then use the full SoloBizKit QR generator if you want to adjust colors, add a logo or download PNG and SVG versions for your card design.

What should the QR code link to?

Choose one clear destination rather than sending people through unnecessary steps. Freelancers may link to a portfolio or booking page, consultants to a contact or service page, and local businesses to a useful landing page. Make sure the destination works well on a phone because most people will scan from a mobile device.

How to print a QR code on a business card

  • Test the QR code at the actual physical size you plan to print.
  • Keep clear white space around the code instead of crowding it with text.
  • Use strong contrast between the QR pattern and its background.
  • Avoid placing the code across folds, edges or highly reflective areas.
  • Scan-test a printed proof on more than one phone before ordering a large quantity.
  • If you add a logo, keep it reasonably small and test the final exported file.

Business card QR code FAQ

What is the best QR code for a business card?

A simple QR code that sends people directly to a useful mobile-friendly page is usually the most practical choice. The destination matters more than adding unnecessary complexity.

Can I make a QR code for my business card for free?

Yes. The current SoloBizKit tool is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account.

How big should the QR code be?

The correct size depends on the card, print quality and scanning conditions. Use a comfortable size, preserve clear space around it and test a physical print at the exact intended dimensions.
