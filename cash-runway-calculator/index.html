How this calculator works

Runway is a planning estimate. Cash flows are rarely perfectly flat, so use realistic timing for major invoices, taxes, payroll and one-off expenses.

Cash runway = current cash balance ÷ monthly net cash burn, where net burn = monthly cash outflow − monthly cash inflow.

Example

$75,000 in cash with an $8,000 monthly net burn provides about 9.4 months of runway.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
