Security & Data Processing

A plain-language overview of what the Pro workspace stores, how account data is separated, which outside services are involved and what users should do to protect customer information.

Last reviewed: September 5, 2026. This page describes the current product architecture. It is not a security certification, audit report or formal data-processing agreement.

What Pro can store

Depending on the features you use, SoloBizKit Pro can store account and profile information, company settings, customer and lead records, CRM status and notes, estimates, invoices, line items, tax rates, recurring invoice profiles, products and services, reminders, payment-status records, customer portal links, attachments and subscription information.

Company settings can include business contact information, tax identifiers, payment terms and payment instructions such as bank account, IBAN or BIC/SWIFT details if you choose to enter them.

Authentication and account access

SoloBizKit Pro currently uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data infrastructure. Session information can be stored in the browser so a signed-in user can remain authenticated between page loads.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials and devices secure. Do not share passwords, and sign out on devices you do not control.

Database access controls

The current Pro database uses user identifiers on workspace records and row-level access policies intended to restrict authenticated users to records that belong to their own account. This includes core areas such as customers, invoices, invoice items, estimates, estimate items, company settings, recurring billing, products, payments and attachments.

Important: access controls reduce risk but do not make any internet service risk-free. SoloBizKit does not claim that a configuration alone guarantees absolute security.

Customer portal links

Customer portal links are designed to expose only the customer-facing information and actions associated with that portal workflow. A valid portal link should be treated like a private access link: send it only to the intended recipient and revoke or replace it when appropriate.

Do not publish portal links publicly or place them in locations where unintended people can access them.

Attachments

Pro can associate files with estimates or invoices. Files and file metadata may therefore be stored in cloud infrastructure rather than only on your device.

SoloBizKit is designed for ordinary small-business documents, not as a specialist repository for medical records, government secrets, authentication credentials, full payment-card data or other highly regulated information. Avoid uploading data that is unnecessary for the business workflow.

Payments and billing

Stripe is used for SoloBizKit Pro subscription checkout and billing. Where Stripe-based invoice payment flows are available, Stripe processes the payment details under its own terms and security controls.

SoloBizKit may retain records needed to show business state, such as subscription status, payment status, provider identifiers, amounts and timestamps. The product is not designed to store raw card numbers or card security codes entered into Stripe-hosted flows.

Public tools and local processing

Many public SoloBizKit calculators and utility tools do not require an account and are designed to process inputs in the browser. Some tools use local browser storage for convenience. Data saved locally is tied to the browser profile and can disappear if site data is cleared.

Browser-based document tools may still load third-party JavaScript libraries from content-delivery networks. A tool-specific notice should be treated as the best description of that tool's current processing behavior.

Third-party services

The service currently relies on third parties for parts of its operation. These can include Supabase for authentication and cloud data infrastructure, Stripe for subscription and payment workflows, hosting and network providers for delivering the site, Google for optional analytics and advertising on public pages, and software/CDN providers used by specific tools.

Third-party services operate under their own security, privacy and availability terms. SoloBizKit does not claim ownership or control over those providers' infrastructure.

Transport and storage claims

Normal production traffic is served through HTTPS-capable web infrastructure and provider-managed services. We intentionally avoid making a blanket custom claim about encryption, geographic data residency, certifications or backup guarantees unless that claim has been separately verified for the relevant provider and configuration.

Retention and deletion

SoloBizKit does not currently publish one fixed retention period that applies to every type of Pro record. Records may remain while an account or business workflow requires them, subject to product controls, operational needs and provider behavior.

Where a delete control is available in the workspace, users can use it for the supported record type. For account-level access, correction or deletion requests, use the contact page. We may need to verify the requesting account before acting on a data request.

Your responsibilities

  • Store only customer information you have a legitimate reason to process.
  • Use accurate recipient addresses before emailing invoices, estimates or portal links.
  • Keep account credentials and private links confidential.
  • Review invoices, estimates, reminders and recurring billing before relying on automation.
  • Do not use SoloBizKit as a substitute for legal, tax, accounting or security advice.

Reporting a security concern

If you believe an account, customer portal link or Pro workflow exposes data incorrectly, stop sharing the affected link where possible and contact us through the contact page with enough detail to reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords, card numbers or unrelated customer secrets in the report.
