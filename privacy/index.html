Privacy Policy

What the site stores, what optional analytics measures, and where third parties are involved.

Last updated: August 17, 2026. This page describes the current site implementation and should be reviewed whenever analytics, advertising or file-processing behavior changes.

Information entered into tools

SoloBizKit does not require an account for its current core tools. Many calculations and generators are designed to run in the browser. We do not intentionally include the values you type into calculator, QR-code, invoice or business-name fields in Google Analytics event parameters.

Different tools can use different browser libraries and processing methods. Do not assume that every future tool has identical privacy behavior; check any tool-specific notice shown on the page.

Files and document tools

Where a document tool explicitly says processing occurs in your browser, the intended workflow is local browser processing. Third-party JavaScript libraries may still be downloaded from content-delivery networks in order for a tool to function. Do not upload or process highly sensitive or regulated material unless you have independently confirmed that the tool is appropriate for that information.

Local storage

The site may use your browser's local storage for preferences, analytics-consent choices and tool convenience features. For example, the invoice generator can keep a draft on the same browser and device. Local storage is not a cloud backup. Clearing browser/site data can remove it, and anyone with access to the same browser profile may potentially access locally saved tool data.

Optional Google Analytics

Our site asks before loading Google Analytics. If you accept, we use analytics to understand aggregate usage such as page visits, which tools are opened and generic actions such as conversion starts or downloads. The analytics implementation is designed not to transmit form contents, filenames, QR contents, invoice contents or search phrases as analytics event values.

If you decline optional analytics, our analytics script does not intentionally load Google Analytics for measurement. Your choice is stored locally so the site can remember it.

Advertising

SoloBizKit includes Google AdSense code on parts of the site. Advertising can involve Google and its partners and may be subject to cookies, device information, consent requirements and regional rules. Advertising consent is not the same thing as our optional Analytics choice.

The current SoloBizKit analytics consent control keeps Google ad-storage signals denied; however, advertising compliance can require additional Google consent-management configuration depending on where ads are served. We review this separately from analytics and do not claim that the small Analytics consent banner alone handles every advertising-consent obligation.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting platform and network providers used to deliver the site may process technical request information such as IP address, user agent, timestamps and requested URLs as part of normal hosting, security and abuse prevention.

External libraries and services

Some tools load software libraries from third-party content-delivery networks. Loading those resources can expose ordinary network-request information to the provider hosting the library. We aim to minimize dependencies and should prefer local/self-hosted assets when it materially improves reliability or privacy.

Sensitive information

Do not enter passwords, authentication secrets, government identifiers, medical records, payment-card data or other highly sensitive information into a general-purpose SoloBizKit tool.

Your choices

You can decline optional Analytics when prompted. You can also clear SoloBizKit site data in your browser to remove locally stored preferences and drafts. Browser privacy controls and content blockers may affect third-party analytics or advertising resources.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates the latest review. Questions or reports about privacy behavior can be sent through our contact page.
