Privacy Policy

How public tools, accounts and the SoloBizKit Pro workspace handle data.

Last updated: September 5, 2026. This page describes the current implementation and should be reviewed when hosting, payments, email, analytics or workspace behavior changes.

Public tools vs. SoloBizKit Pro

SoloBizKit has two different data patterns. Many public calculators and utilities are designed to process inputs in your browser and do not require an account. SoloBizKit Pro is an authenticated cloud workspace, so CRM, estimate, invoice, catalog, reminder and related workspace records are stored remotely so they can persist across sessions.

Account information

When you create or use a Pro account, authentication information such as your email address and account identifiers is processed through our authentication provider. Session information may be stored in your browser so you remain signed in.

CRM and customer data

Pro can store customer and lead information including names, company names, email addresses, phone numbers, CRM status, notes, timestamps and related customer history. This information is stored as part of the signed-in workspace and is intended to be scoped to the account owner.

Estimates, invoices and business settings

Pro can store estimate and invoice numbers, dates, status, currency, line items, quantities, prices, tax rates, notes and customer relationships. Business settings can include business name, contact details, address, tax number, default currency and tax settings, invoice and estimate prefixes, payment terms and payment instructions.

If you enter bank account, IBAN, BIC/SWIFT or other payment instructions into business settings, that information can be stored in your Pro workspace for use on documents. Do not enter information you do not want stored in the cloud.

Recurring billing, reminders and usage records

Pro may store recurring invoice profiles, recurring line items, reminder schedules, invoice-usage records and delivery or activity records needed to support repeat billing and workflow history.

Customer portals and access links

Customer portal features may create access links associated with customer records, invoices or estimates. Those links should be treated as private. Anyone who receives a valid access link may be able to view the information made available through that portal until the link is revoked, expires or is otherwise disabled.

Attachments

Where Pro supports attachments on invoices or estimates, uploaded files and file metadata can be stored with the relevant workspace record. Avoid uploading highly sensitive, regulated or unnecessary personal information unless you have independently confirmed that SoloBizKit is appropriate for that use.

Payments and subscription billing

SoloBizKit uses Stripe for subscription checkout and billing. Where payment links or payment workflows are available for invoices, the payment provider processes payment-card and financial transaction information under its own privacy and security terms. SoloBizKit is not designed to store raw payment-card numbers entered into Stripe-hosted payment flows.

We may store payment-related business records such as payment status, payment identifiers, amounts, timestamps or subscription status when needed to display the state of your workspace.

Public invoice generator and local storage

The free public invoice generator can store a draft locally in your browser. That local draft is separate from a Pro cloud invoice unless you explicitly use a Pro workflow. Local browser storage is not a cloud backup and can be removed by clearing site data.

Files and document tools

Where a document tool explicitly says processing occurs in your browser, the intended workflow is local processing. Third-party JavaScript libraries may still be downloaded from content-delivery networks. Do not process highly sensitive or regulated material unless you have independently confirmed that the specific tool is appropriate.

Optional Google Analytics

Our public site asks before loading optional Google Analytics. If accepted, we use aggregate events such as page visits, tool opens and generic actions. The analytics implementation is designed not to transmit form contents, filenames, QR contents, invoice contents or search phrases as analytics event values.

Advertising

SoloBizKit includes Google AdSense code on parts of the public site. Advertising may involve Google and its partners, cookies, device information and regional consent requirements. Advertising consent is separate from our optional Analytics choice.

Hosting, authentication and data infrastructure

SoloBizKit Pro currently uses Supabase infrastructure for authentication and cloud data storage. Network and hosting providers may process technical request data such as IP address, user agent, timestamps and requested URLs for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

Access controls

Workspace tables are configured with row-level access policies intended to limit authenticated users to their own records. Security controls reduce risk but no internet service can promise absolute security. Keep your password and portal links private and contact us if you believe an account or link has been compromised.

Sensitive information

Do not store passwords, authentication secrets, full payment-card details, government identifiers, medical records or other highly sensitive or regulated information in SoloBizKit unless the service has explicitly stated that such use is supported.

Your choices and data requests

You can decline optional Analytics, clear local browser data and manage your subscription through the available billing controls. For questions about access, correction or deletion of Pro account data, use the contact page.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. See also the Terms of Use and About SoloBizKit.
