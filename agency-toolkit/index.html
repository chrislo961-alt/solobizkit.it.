A practical workflow from numbers to finished work

Use the tools as a sequence instead of isolated calculators.

Separate project and recurring revenueTrack one-off projects separately from retainers or subscriptions so MRR is not inflated by non-recurring work.
Price from delivery economicsEstimate labor and direct delivery costs, then protect the quote with explicit scope and a contingency where uncertainty exists.
Check margin before accepting workRevenue alone can hide an unprofitable account. Review gross or contribution margin using consistent cost definitions.
Standardize billing and follow-upUse consistent invoice numbers, payment terms, due dates and reminder steps so collections do not depend on memory.

Agency Toolkit FAQ

What should an agency include in project pricing?

Include estimated delivery time, direct costs, subcontractors or tools that belong to the project, and a contingency for reasonable scope risk.

What counts as MRR for an agency?

Use recurring contracted monthly revenue that is expected to repeat. One-off projects, setup fees and irregular pass-through costs should normally be tracked separately.

How can an agency improve cash flow?

Consistent deposits, milestones, payment terms, invoice timing and follow-up can reduce the delay between delivery work and cash collection.

Can SoloBizKit replace full agency management software?

SoloBizKit focuses on practical solo and small-business workflows. Larger agencies may still need dedicated project-management, accounting or resource-planning systems.
