Extract specific pages from a PDF

Use SoloBizKit Extract PDF Pages when you only need selected pages from a larger document. Enter single pages, page ranges or a combination of both and create a new PDF containing exactly that selection.

Extract non-consecutive PDF pages

You are not limited to one continuous range. A selection such as 1,4,7-10 extracts page 1, page 4 and pages 7 through 10 into one new PDF.

Keep extracted pages in your chosen order

The output follows the order you enter. For example, 5,2,1 creates a three-page PDF in that sequence. This also makes the tool useful for simple page rearrangement when you only need a subset of the document.

Extract odd or even PDF pages

Quick buttons can generate all odd pages or all even pages automatically. This can help with scanned documents, duplex workflows and documents where every other page needs to be separated.

Extract pages without changing the original PDF

The current tool copies selected pages into a new PDF and leaves the original document unchanged on your device.

Extract PDF Pages FAQ

Can I extract PDF pages for free?

Yes. The current Extract PDF Pages tool is free and does not require a SoloBizKit account.

Can I extract pages 1, 3 and 8 to 12 together?

Yes. Enter 1,3,8-12.

Can I change the output order?

Yes. The output follows the order you enter in the page selection field.

What is the difference between Extract PDF Pages and Split PDF?

Extract PDF Pages is optimized for selecting specific and non-consecutive pages into one new document. Split PDF is better suited to separating a PDF by ranges.
