Remove unwanted pages from a PDF

Use the free SoloBizKit Delete PDF Pages tool when a document contains blank pages, duplicate pages, outdated attachments or sections you do not want to share. Choose exactly which pages to remove and save a new PDF.

Delete single pages or complete page ranges

Enter individual page numbers such as 2, 8, 11 or remove complete ranges such as 5-9. You can also combine both styles in one request.

Remove blank or duplicate PDF pages

If a scan contains accidental blank pages or a document includes duplicate pages, you can remove only those pages without rebuilding the whole PDF. This is useful before sending applications, reports, contracts or client documents.

Keep the original PDF unchanged

The tool creates a new PDF containing the pages you decided to keep. Your original document is not overwritten, so you can compare the result or start again if you remove the wrong page.

Delete pages versus split a PDF

Use Delete PDF Pages when you want to keep most of a document and remove a smaller number of pages. Use Split PDF when you want to extract only selected pages into a separate document.

Can I remove every page?

No. A valid output PDF must contain at least one page, so the tool prevents you from deleting the entire document.

Free PDF page removal without an account

The current tool is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account. Processing is designed to happen in your browser. Password-protected or damaged documents may need to be unlocked or repaired before processing.

Delete PDF pages FAQ

How do I remove pages from a PDF?

Choose the PDF, enter the page numbers or ranges you want to delete and create the cleaned copy.

Can I delete several pages at once?

Yes. Enter a list such as 2, 5-7, 11 to remove separate pages and ranges in the same operation.

Will the original PDF be changed?

No. The tool downloads a new PDF and leaves your original file unchanged.

Can I remove blank pages from a PDF?

Yes. Identify the blank page numbers and enter them in the delete field.
