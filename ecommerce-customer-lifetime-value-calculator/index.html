How to use this calculator

Build averages from a consistent customer group. A recent cohort may not yet reveal its full lifespan, while mixing first-time buyers with loyal subscribers can hide meaningful differences. Use gross margin after product cost, not operating profit margin.

The formula

Revenue CLV = average order value × orders per year × customer lifespan. Gross profit CLV = revenue CLV × gross margin.

Worked example

At a $65 average order value, four orders per year and a three-year lifespan, revenue CLV is $780. With a 55% gross margin, gross profit CLV is $429.

What to include

  • Average order value after normal discounts
  • Repeat purchase frequency over a consistent period
  • A customer lifespan estimate based on retained cohorts
  • Gross margin that reflects product cost

How to interpret the result

This simple model does not discount future cash flow or predict churn month by month. It is most useful for scenario planning and for comparing customer groups using the same method. Compare gross profit CLV—not revenue CLV—with acquisition cost.

Keep definitions consistent when comparing periods. Results depend entirely on the values and assumptions entered.

Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate customer lifespan?

Use historical cohort retention when available. If the store is new, test a conservative range instead of relying on one optimistic value.

Should shipping revenue be in average order value?

Use the same definition as your store reporting and ensure the related shipping cost is reflected in gross margin.

Is CLV the same as total customer revenue?

Revenue CLV is expected revenue. Gross profit CLV applies gross margin to estimate the amount available before acquisition and overhead.
