A simple workflow

  1. Start with your target income and calculate a sustainable freelance rate.
  2. Use that rate consistently when quoting client work.
  3. Create an invoice when the work is complete.
  4. Sign, compress or combine client PDFs when needed.
  5. Review your margin regularly as expenses change.
Good to know: SoloBizKit tools are designed for practical estimates and simple workflows. Requirements for taxes, invoices, registrations and business records can vary by location, so check the rules that apply to you.
