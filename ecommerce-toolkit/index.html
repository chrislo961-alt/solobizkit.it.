A practical workflow from numbers to finished work

Use the tools as a sequence instead of isolated calculators.

Start with unit economicsKnow selling price, product cost, fulfillment and transaction costs before using ad metrics as the primary growth signal.
Measure acquisition efficiencyUse CPC, conversion rate, CAC and ROAS together. One metric alone can make an unprofitable channel look healthy.
Compare CAC with gross-profit customer valueRevenue CLV can overstate what is available to pay for acquisition. Gross-profit CLV gives a more useful planning comparison.
Stress-test pricing and conversionModel price changes and conversion changes separately so you can see which lever has the larger effect on profit.

Ecommerce Toolkit FAQ

Is a high ROAS always profitable?

No. ROAS compares attributed revenue with ad spend. Product cost, fulfillment, platform fees, discounts and returns can still make the campaign unprofitable.

Should CAC be compared with revenue CLV or profit CLV?

Gross-profit CLV is usually the more informative comparison because it accounts for cost of goods sold before acquisition cost is recovered.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup divides profit by cost, while margin divides profit by selling price. They are different percentages even when cost and selling price are the same.

Do these calculators connect directly to Shopify or ad platforms?

No. They are standalone planning tools. Enter totals from the same reporting period and source so the comparison stays consistent.
