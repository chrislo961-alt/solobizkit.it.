Create a professional invoice free online

SoloBizKit keeps the invoice workflow simple: enter your business and client details, add one or more line items, choose currency and payment terms, apply tax or a discount if needed, then review the live invoice preview. When the invoice is ready, use your browser to print it or save it as a PDF.

What can you include on an invoice?

  • Your business name, address, email, phone number and optional logo
  • Client or company name, email and billing address
  • Invoice number, issue date and due date
  • Multiple products, services or billable line items
  • Quantity, rate and automatic line totals
  • Currency, tax percentage and discount percentage
  • Payment terms, notes and payment instructions

Free invoice generator with local auto-save

Your current draft is stored in your browser so an accidental refresh does not immediately erase the invoice. This is convenient for returning to the same device and browser. Local browser storage is not a backup system, so keep your own saved PDF or accounting records for invoices you need to retain.

Save an invoice as PDF

Select Print / Save PDF when the invoice is ready. Your browser or operating system can then provide its normal printing options, including saving to PDF where supported. Review the saved file before sending it to a client.

Invoice numbers, due dates and payment terms

Use a consistent invoice number that makes the document easy to identify later. Add the issue date, due date and payment terms that apply to the agreement with your client. If you need help creating a consistent numbering pattern, use the free Invoice Number Generator.

Invoice requirements vary by country

Tax, VAT, sales-tax, numbering and required invoice fields vary by jurisdiction and business type. SoloBizKit provides a document-building tool, not accounting, tax or legal advice. Make sure your invoice includes the fields required where you operate.

Free invoice generator FAQ

Is the invoice generator really free?

Yes. The current SoloBizKit Invoice Generator is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account.

Can I create an invoice with multiple line items?

Yes. Add as many line items as you need and enter a description, quantity and rate for each one.

Can I add tax and a discount?

Yes. The generator includes percentage fields for tax and discount and calculates the totals automatically.

Can I put my logo on the invoice?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP logo and it will appear in the invoice preview.

Can I save the invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Use Print / Save PDF, then choose the PDF option offered by your browser or operating system.
