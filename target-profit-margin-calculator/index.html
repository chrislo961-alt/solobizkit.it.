About this calculator

Calculate the selling price needed to reach a target gross profit margin. Use it as a quick planning tool, then adjust assumptions in the full SoloBizKit calculator when you need more detail.

Why the inputs matter

Small changes in costs, billable utilization, desired income or selling price can materially change the result. Use realistic assumptions and review them as your business changes.

Target price formula

Target selling price equals cost divided by one minus the target margin written as a decimal. A 100% target margin is impossible because cost cannot be covered when it equals the entire selling price.
