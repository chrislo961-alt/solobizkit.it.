Create invoices free online

An online invoice generator gives you a structured way to create a bill for products, services, freelance work or other customer transactions. Instead of formatting a document from scratch, enter the invoice details, review the calculations and create a consistent document for the customer.

What can the full SoloBizKit Invoice Generator do?

  • Add your business name, contact information and optional logo.
  • Add the customer or client's billing details.
  • Set an invoice number, issue date and due date.
  • Create multiple product or service line items.
  • Enter quantity or hours and a separate rate for each item.
  • Select common currencies.
  • Apply percentage tax and discounts.
  • Add payment terms, notes and payment instructions.
  • Preview the finished invoice while you work.
  • Print or save the invoice as PDF using supported browser options.

How to create an invoice online

Start by identifying your business and the customer. Add a unique or otherwise appropriate invoice number, issue date and due date. List the products or services being billed, calculate the totals and applicable tax, then add payment terms and instructions. Review everything before sending the invoice. Our How to Make an Invoice guide explains the workflow step by step.

Print or save invoices as PDF

When your invoice is complete, choose Print / Save PDF in the full generator. Your browser or operating system can then provide its normal print options, including PDF saving where supported. Review the generated file before sending it to the customer.

Invoice data and local auto-save

The current full generator stores the working invoice draft locally in your browser to reduce the risk of losing work after an accidental refresh. Local browser storage is not a permanent accounting archive, so retain your own invoice records and saved files according to the requirements that apply to your business.

Invoice requirements vary by country

Invoice numbering, required seller and customer information, tax or VAT fields, sales-tax treatment and record-retention rules vary by jurisdiction, business type and transaction. SoloBizKit helps build the invoice document but does not determine your accounting, tax or legal obligations.

Free online invoice generator FAQ

Is the SoloBizKit Invoice Generator free?

Yes. The current invoice generator is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes. The full invoice generator supports an optional business logo in PNG, JPEG or WebP format.

Can I invoice more than one service or product?

Yes. Add multiple line items and enter a separate description, quantity and rate for each.

Can I add tax and discounts?

Yes. The full generator includes percentage tax and discount fields and updates the totals automatically.

Can I download the invoice as PDF?

Use Print / Save PDF in the full generator and choose the PDF save option provided by your browser or operating system where supported.
