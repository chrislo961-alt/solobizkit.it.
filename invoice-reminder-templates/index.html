INVOICE WORKFLOW GUIDE

Invoice reminder templates

A good payment reminder is short, specific and easy to act on. Include the invoice number, amount, due date and a clear next step.

Before the due date

Subject: Invoice [number] due [date]

Hi [name], a quick reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due on [date]. Payment details are included on the invoice. Thanks.

First overdue reminder

Subject: Invoice [number] is now overdue

Hi [name], invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date]. Could you confirm when payment is scheduled? I’ve attached/relinked the invoice for convenience. Thanks.

Second overdue reminder

Subject: Follow-up: overdue invoice [number]

Hi [name], I’m following up on invoice [number], which remains unpaid. Please confirm the payment date or let me know if there is an issue with the invoice that needs resolving.

Keep the workflow organized
