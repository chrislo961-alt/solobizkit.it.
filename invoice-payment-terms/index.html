INVOICE WORKFLOW GUIDE

Invoice payment terms explained

Payment terms tell a client when an invoice is due and what happens if payment is late. Clear terms reduce ambiguity before the invoice leaves your inbox.

Common payment terms

  • Due on receipt: payment is requested immediately.
  • Net 7: due 7 calendar days after the invoice date.
  • Net 14: due 14 calendar days after the invoice date.
  • Net 30: due 30 calendar days after the invoice date.
  • Net 60: due 60 calendar days after the invoice date.

What to write on the invoice

State the due date, accepted payment method, payment reference and any late-payment terms that were agreed before the work. Avoid vague wording such as “pay soon” when a specific date is possible.

Before charging a late fee

Late-payment rules vary by contract and jurisdiction. Do not add a fee simply because a payment is overdue; check the agreement and applicable law first. The Late Invoice Fee Calculator is a planning tool, not a legal determination.
