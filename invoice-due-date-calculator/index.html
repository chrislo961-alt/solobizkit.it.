How this calculator works

Payment terms can use calendar days, business days or a fixed date depending on the agreement. Confirm the convention used with your client.

Due date = invoice issue date + agreed payment term in calendar days.

Example

An invoice issued on October 1 with Net 30 terms is due 30 calendar days later.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
