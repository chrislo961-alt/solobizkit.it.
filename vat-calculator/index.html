How to use the VAT Calculator

Choose Add VAT when you know the net price before VAT. Choose Remove VAT when you have a VAT-inclusive total and want to find the net amount and VAT portion. Select a common VAT rate or enter your own custom percentage.

VAT formulas

Add VAT: VAT = net amount × VAT rate ÷ 100. Total = net amount + VAT.

Remove VAT: net amount = VAT-inclusive total ÷ (1 + VAT rate ÷ 100). VAT = total − net amount.

Example

With a net amount of $1,000 and a 25% VAT rate, VAT is $250 and the VAT-inclusive total is $1,250. If you remove 25% VAT from a $1,250 gross total, the net amount is $1,000.

Questions people ask

How do I add 25% VAT?

Multiply the net amount by 0.25 to find the VAT, then add that VAT amount to the net price.

Why can’t I remove VAT by simply subtracting 25%?

Because the VAT percentage is calculated from the net amount, not from the VAT-inclusive total. Divide the gross total by 1.25 to remove 25% VAT correctly.

Can I use another VAT rate?

Yes. Choose one of the common rates or select Custom and enter the percentage you need.
