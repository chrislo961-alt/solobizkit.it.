How this calculator works

Only apply fees that are permitted by your contract and applicable law. This calculator does not determine whether a fee is enforceable or whether tax applies.

Late fee = overdue balance × late fee rate × number of fee periods.

Example

A $2,500 overdue balance with a 1.5% monthly late fee for two months produces an estimated $75 late fee and a revised balance of $2,575.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
