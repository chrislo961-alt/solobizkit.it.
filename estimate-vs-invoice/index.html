INVOICE WORKFLOW GUIDE

Estimate vs invoice

An estimate describes expected work and price before the work is finalized. An invoice requests payment for work, products or services that are billable.

Estimate

An estimate is used before or during the sales process. It normally describes scope, expected price, assumptions, validity period and any exclusions.

Invoice

An invoice is a payment document. It normally includes seller and client details, invoice number, dates, line items, totals, payment terms and payment instructions.

Typical workflow

  1. Discuss scope and requirements.
  2. Create an estimate or quote.
  3. Client accepts the work.
  4. Complete the agreed work or billing milestone.
  5. Convert the approved amount into an invoice.
  6. Track payment and follow up if needed.
