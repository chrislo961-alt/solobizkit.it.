How this calculator works

A pricing estimate should reflect scope risk, revisions, payment timing and value to the client. Use a written scope so extra work is handled consistently.

Project price = (estimated hours × hourly rate + direct costs) × (1 + contingency percentage).

Example

40 hours at $95 plus $300 in direct costs and a 15% contingency produces an estimated project price of $4,715.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
