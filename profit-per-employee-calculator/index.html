How this calculator works

Use average headcount for the same reporting period as profit and revenue. Contractors and part-time staff can be included consistently using full-time-equivalent headcount when appropriate.

Profit per employee = annual profit ÷ average employee count.

Example

$240,000 in annual profit across an average team of 12 equals $20,000 profit per employee.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
