How to calculate return on investment

ROI compares the profit generated by an investment with what the investment cost. It is useful for comparing projects, marketing campaigns, equipment or process changes when the underlying inputs are measured consistently.

ROI = (total return − investment cost) ÷ investment cost × 100

Simple ROI vs annualized ROI

Simple ROI shows the total percentage return for the period you enter. Annualized ROI converts that result into an equivalent compound yearly rate. Annualizing can make different time periods easier to compare, but it does not mean the same return will repeat every year.

What should count as investment cost?

Include the relevant cash outlay and any implementation costs needed to generate the return. For a marketing campaign, that could include ad spend, creative production and agency fees. For equipment, it could include purchase, delivery, installation and training.

What is a good ROI?

There is no universal good percentage. A useful comparison includes risk, timing, financing cost, alternative uses of the money and the reliability of the expected return. A high forecast based on uncertain revenue may be less attractive than a lower return backed by repeatable savings.

ROI calculator FAQ

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. When the total return is lower than the investment cost, net profit and ROI are negative.

Does this calculator include tax or inflation?

No. This is a general planning calculator. Add those effects to the values you enter when they are relevant to your decision.
