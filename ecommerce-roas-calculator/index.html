How to use this calculator

Use revenue attributed under one consistent model and the matching ad spend. Enter gross margin after product cost and other direct product costs. If discounts, returns or shipping subsidies are material, make sure the margin reflects them.

The formula

ROAS = attributed revenue ÷ ad spend. Contribution after ads = attributed revenue × gross margin − ad spend. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin rate.

Worked example

With $18,000 in attributed revenue, $4,000 in ad spend and a 55% gross margin, ROAS is 4.5× and contribution after ad spend is $5,900 before overhead.

What to include

  • Net attributed revenue after expected returns
  • The full media cost for the same campaigns and dates
  • Gross margin based on the advertised product mix
  • Consistent attribution windows across comparisons

How to interpret the result

A positive contribution after ads does not include salaries, software, rent or other overhead. ROAS can also look different under click-based and view-based attribution. Use the same method when comparing channels and time periods.

Keep definitions consistent when comparing periods. Results depend entirely on the values and assumptions entered.

Frequently asked questions

What is break-even ROAS?

It is the ROAS at which gross profit from attributed revenue equals ad spend, before overhead.

Why is gross margin needed?

ROAS uses revenue, but revenue is not profit. Gross margin estimates what remains after direct product cost.

Should refunds be removed from revenue?

Yes when possible. Net attributed revenue gives a more realistic view of campaign economics.
