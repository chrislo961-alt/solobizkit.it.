Add a text watermark to every PDF page

Use the free SoloBizKit Watermark PDF tool when a document needs a clear status or usage label. Add text such as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, SAMPLE, INTERNAL, COPY or another custom message to every page.

Mark PDFs as confidential, draft or sample

Text watermarks can help communicate document status at a glance. They are useful for internal drafts, sample documents, review copies, previews and files that should be treated as confidential.

Choose watermark position and opacity

Place the watermark diagonally across the center or use a straight watermark at the top or bottom of each page. Light, medium and strong opacity options let you balance visibility with readability of the underlying document.

Add a watermark without converting PDF pages to images

The current tool draws text directly onto the PDF pages rather than intentionally rasterizing the document. That helps preserve the underlying PDF page content while adding a visible mark.

Does watermarking change the original PDF?

No. The tool creates a new watermarked PDF and leaves the original file unchanged on your device.

Free PDF watermarking without an account

The current Watermark PDF tool is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account. Processing is designed to happen in your browser. Password-protected or damaged PDFs may need to be unlocked or repaired before a watermark can be added.

Watermark PDF FAQ

Can I add CONFIDENTIAL to every PDF page?

Yes. Enter CONFIDENTIAL as the watermark text and it will be applied across all pages.

Can I use my own watermark text?

Yes. You can enter up to 60 characters, including labels such as DRAFT, SAMPLE, INTERNAL or a custom message.

Can I change how visible the watermark is?

Yes. Choose light, medium or strong opacity.

Can I add an image or logo watermark?

The current Watermark PDF tool supports text watermarks only. For other PDF modifications, see Edit PDF.
