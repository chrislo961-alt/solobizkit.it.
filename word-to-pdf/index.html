Convert DOCX Word documents to PDF

Use the free SoloBizKit Word to PDF converter when you need a shareable or printable version of a DOCX document. PDF is useful when you want the recipient to read or print a document without editing the original Word file.

Best for simple Word documents

This browser converter works best with ordinary text documents containing headings, paragraphs, lists and simple tables. Word files can contain complex layout features, so pixel-perfect reproduction is not guaranteed.

Word to PDF for letters, reports and simple documents

The converter is most useful for straightforward DOCX files such as letters, short reports, notes, applications and text-heavy business documents. Review the downloaded PDF carefully before sending anything important.

Why Word and PDF can look different

Microsoft Word and PDF use different layout systems. Headers, floating shapes, embedded fonts, unusual page breaks and some images may shift during browser conversion. Always review the downloaded PDF before using it for contracts, applications or other important documents.

DOCX versus DOC support

The current browser tool supports modern .docx files. It does not support the older .doc format. If your document is an older DOC file, save or export it as DOCX first and then try the converter.

Free Word to PDF conversion without an account

The current Word to PDF converter is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account. Conversion is designed to happen in your browser rather than intentionally sending the document contents to a SoloBizKit server.

Word to PDF FAQ

How do I convert Word to PDF?

Choose a DOCX file, click Convert Word to PDF and review the downloaded PDF before using it.

Will my Word formatting be preserved?

Simple text formatting usually converts best. Complex headers, page layouts, fonts, floating objects and advanced Word features may shift.

Can I convert old DOC files?

Not with the current version. Save the file as DOCX first and then run the conversion.

Can I convert a PDF back to Word?

Yes. Use the free PDF to Word converter for selectable PDF text.
