FAQ

Before you start.

Is the free toolkit going away?

No. SoloBizKit’s public calculators and utility tools remain free. Pro is the private business workspace for CRM, estimates and invoicing.

Do I need a card for the trial?

Yes. Stripe collects a payment method when you start the subscription. Eligible new accounts are not charged until the 14-day trial ends.

Can I cancel during the trial?

Yes. Subscription and payment details are managed securely through the Stripe billing portal.

Is this accounting software?

No. SoloBizKit Pro focuses on customer management, quoting, invoicing and getting paid rather than full bookkeeping or payroll.
