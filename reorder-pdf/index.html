Rearrange PDF pages with drag and drop

Use the free SoloBizKit Reorder PDF Pages tool when pages are in the wrong sequence. After the PDF loads, each page appears as a thumbnail that you can drag into a new position.

Fix PDF page order before sharing

Reordering can be useful for scanned documents, reports, manuals, applications, statements and merged files where pages were added in the wrong order.

Reverse the order of a PDF

If the whole document is backwards, use Reverse order to flip the page sequence instantly, then download the corrected copy.

Reorder pages without changing page content

The tool copies the original PDF pages into a new document in your chosen sequence. It does not intentionally rasterize pages or rewrite the content on each page.

Browser-based PDF page organization

The current tool is designed to load page previews and rebuild the PDF in your browser. Your original file remains unchanged on your device.

Reorder PDF FAQ

Yes. The current tool is free and does not require a SoloBizKit account.

Can I drag pages into a new order?

Yes. Drag page thumbnails and drop them where you want them in the sequence.

Can I reverse all pages?

Yes. Use the Reverse order button.
