How this calculator works

Taxes, insurance, retirement, paid leave and local obligations vary. Include the costs that actually apply to your business.

Target contractor rate = (income target + business costs) × (1 + buffer) ÷ annual billable hours.

Example

An $80,000 income target, $18,000 in business costs, 1,200 billable hours and a 10% buffer requires about $89.83 per billable hour.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
