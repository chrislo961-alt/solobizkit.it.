About this hourly paycheck calculator

Estimate take-home pay from an hourly wage and weekly hours, with payroll taxes and deductions. This focused version uses a simplified withholding model and the 2026 employee Social Security and standard Medicare rates.

What can change the real paycheck?

  • Form W-4 elections and filing circumstances.
  • State and local tax rules.
  • Benefits, retirement contributions and other deductions.
  • Bonuses, overtime and multiple jobs.
