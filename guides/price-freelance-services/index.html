Pricing

How to price freelance services without guessing

Start with the revenue your business needs, divide by realistic billable time, then adjust the project price for scope, risk, speed and value.

Calculate a sustainable floor

Use annual income target + annual business costs + buffer, then divide by realistic billable hours.

Example

You want 45,000 in personal income, expect 10,000 in business costs and want a 5,000 buffer. Revenue target = 60,000. If you can realistically bill 1,000 hours per year, your planning floor is 60 per billable hour before taxes and other local obligations.

Turn the floor into a project price

Estimate internal hours.

Include calls, revisions, admin, testing and handover.

Add direct costs.

Licenses, subcontractors, travel or paid assets.

Add risk.

Control scope before discounting

If the client cannot afford the price, reduce scope instead of randomly cutting the number. Remove deliverables, reduce revisions, extend turnaround or split work into phases.

The client says the quote is too expensive. Current scope: [scope]. Current price: [price]. Give me 3 lower-cost versions by reducing scope, not by discounting the same work. Show exactly what is removed from each option.
