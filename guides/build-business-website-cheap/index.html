How to build a business website almost free

You do not need to be a developer. With an AI assistant, a domain and free static hosting, an ordinary person can build a clean business website from scratch and get it online in an afternoon.

Hands-on guideNo coding experience requiredReviewed Sep 5, 2026
The practical version: you can ask an AI assistant to plan the pages, write the copy, generate the HTML/CSS, explain every technical step and help fix errors. You still decide what is true about your business and review what gets published.

1. Choose the easiest route for you

Route A: AI + simple website builder

Best if you want drag-and-drop editing. Use AI for structure, copy, FAQs, SEO titles and design direction, then paste the result into your builder.

Route B: AI + static HTML

Best if you want near-zero hosting cost and full control. Ask AI to create the files, upload them to GitHub and publish with GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages.

For a normal local service business, consultant or freelancer, both routes can produce a professional site. The important part is not the technology. It is whether a customer can understand what you do and contact you.

2. Plan the site before you generate anything

Write down six facts. Do not make them sound impressive yet — just make them accurate.

Do this now

Open an AI assistant and paste the prompt below. Replace the brackets with your own business information.

I am building a simple website for [business type] in [location/market]. We sell: [service/product] Ideal customer: [customer] Main problem we solve: [problem] Proof we have: [experience/reviews/examples] Primary action I want visitors to take: [call/email/book/request quote] Create a simple website plan for a small business. Keep it practical and trustworthy. Give me: 1. the recommended pages, 2. the sections on the home page, 3. a clear headline and subheadline, 4. the information I still need to collect, 5. anything that would make the site look untrustworthy if missing. Do not invent facts, customers, reviews or credentials.

3. Start smaller than you think

A first version can be one page. A useful structure is:

Hero.

One sentence explaining what you do, who it is for and the main next step.

Services.

Three to six clear services or outcomes. Avoid vague labels such as “solutions” unless you explain them.

Why choose you.

Real proof: experience, process, warranty, location, certifications, response time or examples.

How it works.

Explain the customer journey in three or four steps.

FAQ.

Answer the questions people ask before contacting you.

Contact.

Phone, email, form or booking link. Make the next step obvious.

4. Let AI create the first version

If you choose the static HTML route, you can have AI create the complete starter site. You do not need to understand every line before you begin.

Build me a complete responsive one-page business website using only HTML, CSS and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript if needed. Business: [name] Type: [business type] Location/market: [location] Services: [services] Main CTA: [call/email/request quote] Style: clean, modern, professional, simple Requirements: - mobile friendly - accessible contrast and readable font sizes - fast loading - no frameworks - no fake testimonials - no invented statistics - sections for hero, services, why us, process, FAQ and contact - title tag and meta description - one H1 - semantic headings - simple navigation - clear CTA buttons - placeholder comments showing where I must insert real business details Give me the complete index.html and styles.css files, then explain exactly how to preview them on my computer.

When AI returns the files, save them in one folder. Open index.html in your browser. You already have a website — it is simply not public yet.

Use AI like a patient technical helper

If something looks wrong, take a screenshot and ask for a specific fix. Useful requests include:

The mobile menu overlaps the headline. Here is a screenshot. Tell me what is causing it and give me the exact code change. Make the page feel more professional without adding animations or extra sections. Reduce the AI-generated feeling. Make the wording shorter, more specific and more like a real local business. Check this HTML for broken links, missing alt text, duplicate H1 tags and obvious SEO mistakes.

5. Improve the copy instead of accepting the first AI draft

AI is useful for a first draft, but generic copy is easy to spot. Replace “we deliver innovative solutions” with facts that only your business could honestly say.

WeakBetter
Quality solutions tailored to your needsBathroom renovation for homeowners in Karlstad, from demolition to final finish
Customer satisfaction is our priorityOne contact person from quote to completed job
Contact us todaySend photos of the job and we will tell you what information we need for a quote

6. Publish it with free static hosting

For an HTML site, one common workflow is GitHub plus a static host. The exact menus change over time, but the process is simple:

Create a GitHub account and repository.

Create a repository for the website. AI can explain every field if you are unsure.

Upload your files.

Add index.html, styles.css and your images. Keep filenames simple and lowercase.

Choose hosting.

GitHub Pages can publish directly from the repository. Cloudflare Pages and similar services can connect to a repository and deploy automatically when files change.

Open the temporary URL.

Test the public version before connecting a paid domain.

If GitHub feels technical

Copy the text shown on the screen into your AI assistant and ask: “Tell me exactly what to click next. Assume I have never used GitHub before.” That is a perfectly valid way to learn this workflow.

7. Buy and connect a domain

A domain is the address people type, such as yourbusiness.com. Buy it from a reputable registrar. You normally connect it to the host by adding DNS records supplied by the hosting provider.

You do not need to memorize DNS. Copy the exact instructions or screenshot into an AI assistant and ask it to explain which record belongs in which field. Do not share passwords, recovery codes or private API keys.

Domain checklist

  • Short and easy to spell
  • No unnecessary hyphens or unusual spelling
  • Close to the real business name
  • Auto-renew enabled if losing the domain would be costly
  • Account protected with multi-factor authentication

8. Add the boring things that make the site trustworthy

Real businesses need more than a nice hero section. Depending on what the site does, add your real contact details, About information, Privacy information, terms where relevant, and clear explanations of forms, analytics, payments or subscriptions.

Do not ask AI to invent legal compliance. It can help you organize and explain your real setup, but privacy, tax, accessibility and industry-specific requirements still need to match your actual business and jurisdiction.

9. Basic SEO a beginner can do

Act as an SEO editor for a real small business website. Review the page text below. Suggest improvements for clarity, search intent and usefulness without keyword stuffing. Do not invent locations, services, credentials or customer claims. Give me a revised title, meta description, H1, section headings and any missing customer questions.

10. Run a real launch test

Do not launch because the page “looks finished.” Test what a customer will actually do.

What this can realistically cost

If you use free static hosting and do the work yourself with AI assistance, the domain may be the only unavoidable recurring website cost. Paid email, forms, booking, ecommerce or premium builders can add costs later. Start with free or low-cost infrastructure and pay when a real requirement justifies it.

Your first 90 minutes

Spend 15 minutes writing the six business facts, 20 minutes using AI to plan and draft the site, 30 minutes generating or building the first version, and the rest testing and correcting it. Do not spend the first day choosing animations, logo effects or a perfect font.

Platform pricing, free tiers and interfaces change. Check current provider terms before relying on a specific feature or price. Never paste passwords, recovery codes, private keys or customer-sensitive data into an AI prompt.
