How to set up a simple CRM for a small business

A useful CRM answers three questions fast: who is this customer, what happened last, and what should I do next?

Start with eight fields

FieldWhy it matters
NameWho you are dealing with
CompanyCommercial context
Email / phoneContact method
StatusWhere they are in the pipeline
Last contactWhat happened recently
Next actionWhat you must do next
Next dateWhen to do it
NotesRelevant commercial context

Use a tiny pipeline

Start with Lead → Contacted → Active opportunity → Client → Won/Lost/Archived. Add stages only when they change what you actually do next.

Example

“Active opportunity” means there is a real need, a conversation has happened and a next commercial step exists. If no next step exists, the CRM should make that obvious.

You can start in a spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is enough if you have a small number of leads and one person managing them. The point is consistency, not software.

Create a simple CRM spreadsheet structure for a one-person [business type] business. I need to track leads, customers, quotes and follow-ups. Give me: - column names - allowed status values - a simple follow-up rule - which fields should be mandatory - which fields I should avoid collecting unless necessary Keep it simple enough to maintain in under 10 minutes per day.

Set follow-up rules before you need them

New lead.

Assign an owner/status and a next action immediately.

After first contact.

Set a specific follow-up date instead of “remembering later.”

After sending an estimate.

Record sent date, amount, status and next check-in.

After a win.

Move the customer into delivery/invoice workflow.

After a loss.

Record why if known, then archive cleanly.

Use AI to clean notes, not expose sensitive data

Turn these rough sales notes into a concise CRM update with four headings: - Current need - What happened - Next action - Open questions Do not add facts. Keep it under 120 words. Notes: [paste non-sensitive notes]
Do not paste passwords, identity documents, payment-card data, health data or other highly sensitive customer information into an AI assistant just to tidy CRM notes.

Create a daily CRM habit

When to move beyond a spreadsheet

Move when you repeatedly miss follow-ups, duplicate customer details, need linked estimates/invoices, want activity history or spend too much time maintaining the sheet itself.
