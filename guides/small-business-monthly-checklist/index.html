Why this routine works

Small businesses often fail operationally through accumulation: one invoice not chased, one lead forgotten, one subscription left running, one missing backup. A monthly review gives these small issues a fixed place to be handled.

Put this review on the calendar for the same day every month. A routine you actually complete is better than a perfect checklist you rarely open.
