Monthly operations

Small business monthly checklist

A 45–60 minute monthly review can catch overdue invoices, weak cash flow, forgotten leads, broken website flows and unnecessary costs before they become bigger problems.

Block one recurring admin hour

Put the review on the same day each month. Open your bank/cash view, invoice list, CRM, subscriptions and website before you begin so the review stays operational instead of theoretical.

15 minutes: money.

Review unpaid invoices, upcoming bills, cash position and major commitments.

15 minutes: customers.

Review open leads, estimates, follow-ups and inactive opportunities.

15 minutes: systems.

Test key website flows, backups, subscriptions and access.

15 minutes: decisions.

Choose the three most important actions for the next month.
