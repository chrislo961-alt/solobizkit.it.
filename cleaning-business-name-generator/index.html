How to choose a good cleaning name

A strong name is usually easy to say, easy to spell and distinctive enough to remember. For a cleaning business, it should also match the level of trust, personality and professionalism customers expect.

Before you register a name

  • Check relevant trademark databases.
  • Check your state or country business registry.
  • Look for a suitable domain name.
  • Check important social media handles.
  • Avoid names that are confusingly similar to competitors.
