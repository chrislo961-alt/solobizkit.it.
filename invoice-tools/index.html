A simple invoice workflow

Keep the process repeatable so every invoice does not start from zero.

1. Agree scope and priceUse an estimate or written scope before work begins when price or deliverables need approval.
2. Create the invoiceUse a consistent number, issue date, due date, line items and payment instructions.
3. Save your recordKeep the PDF and any supporting agreement in your own records. Browser local storage is convenient but is not a backup system.
4. Follow up consistentlyIf payment is late, reference the invoice number, amount and due date and ask for a specific payment date.

When should you use SoloBizKit Pro?

The free tools are designed for one-off tasks. SoloBizKit Pro becomes useful when you want customer history, estimates, recurring invoices, reminders and payment status stored together in one workspace.

Invoice tools FAQ

Are the invoice tools free?

Yes. The core invoice generator, calculators and guides can be used without creating a SoloBizKit account.

Can I save an invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Use the invoice generator's Print / Save PDF action and choose the PDF option offered by your browser or operating system.

Does SoloBizKit decide which tax fields I need?

No. Tax, VAT, sales-tax, numbering and required invoice fields vary by jurisdiction and business type. Verify the requirements that apply where you operate.

Can I charge a late fee?

That depends on your agreement and applicable rules. The late-fee calculator only performs arithmetic; it does not determine whether a fee is allowed or enforceable.
