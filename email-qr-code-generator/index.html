When is an email QR code useful?

Put it on printed brochures, product packaging, signs, business cards or event materials when you want people to contact you without typing an email address.

What happens after scanning?

The QR code contains a standard mailto link. A compatible phone normally opens its default email application. The visitor can review and edit the email before sending it.
