Are SoloBizKit tools free?

Yes. Current SoloBizKit tools are free to use and do not require a SoloBizKit account.

Which business calculators are available?

Use calculators for profit margin, break-even, ROI, business loans, cash flow, markup and freelance rates.

Are the results financial, tax or legal advice?

No. Calculator results are planning estimates based on the inputs and assumptions shown. Verify important decisions against your records and applicable professional guidance.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

Many PDF tools process files in the browser. Check the privacy note on each tool because browser limits and implementation can differ by task.
