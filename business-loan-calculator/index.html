Estimate a business loan payment

A fixed-rate installment loan normally spreads principal and interest across equal scheduled monthly payments. Early payments contain more interest because the outstanding balance is larger; later payments contain more principal.

Payment = principal × monthly rate × (1 + rate)months ÷ ((1 + rate)months − 1)

How extra payments affect a business loan

An extra payment reduces principal sooner, so less interest accrues in later months. Confirm that your lender applies extra money to principal and does not charge a prepayment penalty before relying on projected savings.

Costs this calculator does not include

Origination fees, guarantees, broker fees, legal costs, insurance, late fees and variable-rate changes are not included automatically. Compare annual percentage rate or the lender's full repayment schedule when evaluating a real offer.

Business loan calculator FAQ

What interest rate should I enter?

Use the stated nominal annual rate for a fixed-rate loan. If the offer gives only an APR, the result can differ because APR may include fees.

Can I use this for a line of credit?

Not reliably. A revolving credit line can have changing balances, draw dates and variable rates that need a different model.
