How this calculator works

Markup and margin are not interchangeable. Use the metric your pricing process requires and keep taxes, fees and variable costs consistent.

Margin = profit ÷ selling price. Markup = profit ÷ cost. Profit = selling price − cost.

Example

A $60 cost sold for $100 creates $40 profit, a 40% margin and a 66.7% markup.

What the result means

The main result is calculated directly from the numbers you enter. It is not an AI estimate. Use it to test scenarios, then adjust the inputs to reflect your actual business.
