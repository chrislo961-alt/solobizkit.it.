About this calculator

Calculate markup percentage, profit and selling price from cost and price. Use it as a quick planning tool, then adjust assumptions in the full SoloBizKit calculator when you need more detail.

Why the inputs matter

Small changes in costs, billable utilization, desired income or selling price can materially change the result. Use realistic assumptions and review them as your business changes.

Markup is not margin

Markup compares profit with cost, while margin compares profit with selling price. A product that costs 40 and sells for 100 has a 150% markup but a 60% profit margin.
