Generate brandable business name ideas

SoloBizKit combines words from your description, industry, brand style and optional keyword to create a range of business name ideas. Use the shortlist to keep favorites and refine individual names into more variations.

Are the names available to register?

Not necessarily. Before using a name commercially, check relevant trademark databases, company registries, social handles and domains in the markets where you operate.

Can I check domain availability?

Each name includes a domain-search button that opens a search for the name. Domain availability changes constantly, so it should be checked at the time you are ready to register.

Where are saved names stored?

Your shortlist is stored in local browser storage on your current device. It is not backed up to a SoloBizKit account.
