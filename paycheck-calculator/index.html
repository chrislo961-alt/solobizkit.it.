Estimate your 2026 take-home pay

This calculator converts salary or hourly wages into gross pay per paycheck, then estimates payroll taxes and subtracts the withholding rates and deductions you enter. It is designed for quick planning rather than preparing an actual payroll or tax return.

What Social Security rate does this calculator use?

For 2026, it uses the employee Social Security rate of 6.2% on covered wages up to the annual Social Security wage base of $184,500.

What Medicare rate does it use?

It uses the standard employee Medicare rate of 1.45%. It also estimates the 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax above the applicable wage-withholding threshold for the selected filing-status context, but actual tax liability can differ.

Why can I adjust federal and state withholding manually?

Actual withholding depends on W-4 elections, filing circumstances, credits, multiple jobs, state and local rules, and other factors. A transparent adjustable estimate is more appropriate for this general calculator than presenting a generic tax result as exact.
