Convert PDF files to editable Word documents

Use the free SoloBizKit PDF to Word converter when you need selectable PDF text in an editable DOCX file. The tool extracts text from each PDF page and rebuilds it into a Word document you can edit after download.

Convert multiple PDFs to Word at once

Add several PDF files in one upload. Choose separate output when each PDF should become its own DOCX, or combined output when you want the extracted text from several PDFs placed into one Word document in the same file order.

What is preserved during PDF to Word conversion?

Page breaks are preserved between PDF pages and selectable text is extracted into Word paragraphs. PDF and Word use very different layout systems, so the original design is not reproduced pixel-for-pixel.

Important: complex layouts, scanned pages, forms, tables, columns and graphics may not reproduce exactly. Image-only scanned PDFs need OCR, which this version does not perform.

PDF to Word for editing text

This converter works best when the PDF contains normal selectable text. If the PDF is mostly scanned images, screenshots or photographed pages, the output may contain little or no usable text because OCR is not included.

Free PDF to Word conversion without an account

The current converter is free to use and does not require a SoloBizKit account. Conversion is designed to happen in your browser rather than intentionally uploading the document contents to a SoloBizKit server.

PDF to Word FAQ

Can I convert more than one PDF at the same time?

Yes. Add multiple PDFs and choose separate Word files or one combined DOCX.

Can PDF to Word preserve complex formatting?

Not perfectly. The current tool focuses on extracting selectable text, so advanced layout, graphics, forms and complex tables may change or disappear.

Does this tool use OCR?

No. Image-only scanned PDFs require OCR and are not converted into editable text by this version.

Can I convert a Word file back to PDF?

Yes. Use the free Word to PDF converter.
