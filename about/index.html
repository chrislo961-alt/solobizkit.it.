How SoloBizKit works

Useful tools, with the assumptions visible.

SoloBizKit is a collection of practical browser tools for freelancers and small businesses. We aim to make simple jobs fast without hiding important limitations.

Last reviewed: August 17, 2026

What we build

The toolkit currently includes document conversion, QR-code generators, invoicing helpers, pricing and profit calculators, paycheck estimators and business-name tools. We prioritize tools that can solve a concrete task in a few minutes.

Use before signupCore tools are designed to work without forcing you to create an account.
Explain the formulaCalculators should state their basic assumptions and what the result represents.
Prefer browser processingWhere practical, generation and calculations happen in your browser rather than sending tool inputs to our own server.
Say when a tool is only an estimatePlanning calculators are not a substitute for tax, accounting, legal or payroll advice.

Calculation methodology

Our general business calculators use straightforward arithmetic based on the values you enter. For example, profit margin is calculated from selling price and cost; break-even units are based on fixed costs divided by contribution per unit; hourly-rate planning divides an income-and-cost target by estimated billable hours. Each calculator should explain its specific formula on the tool page.

Results can change materially when real-world factors are added, including taxes, fees, refunds, discounts, non-billable work, changing costs and local rules. We therefore label these tools as planning estimates where appropriate.

Paycheck and tax-related tools

Payroll and tax rules vary by country, region, filing status and tax year. A generic paycheck estimate should not be treated as an authoritative payroll or tax calculation. Where a SoloBizKit tool does not model the rules that apply to you, the tool should say so clearly.

Document conversion

PDF files can contain text, images, scans, tables, unusual fonts and complex layouts. Browser-based conversion may extract useful editable content without perfectly reproducing the original layout. Scanned documents may require OCR, and complex files may need a specialist converter.

Privacy approach

We avoid intentionally sending values typed into calculator, QR, invoice or business-name fields as Google Analytics event parameters. Some tools use local browser storage for convenience, such as keeping an invoice draft on the same device. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

Analytics and improvement

If a visitor accepts optional analytics, we use aggregate events such as opening a tool, starting a conversion or using a download action. This helps us decide which tools deserve improvement. We intentionally track the action rather than the content entered into the tool.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a broken tool, unclear assumption or incorrect explanation, please use the contact page. Clear limitations and corrections are more useful than pretending every simple calculator covers every real-world situation.
