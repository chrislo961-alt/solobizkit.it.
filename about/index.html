How SoloBizKit works

Free tools for quick jobs. Pro for running the workflow.

SoloBizKit combines practical public tools with an optional private workspace for customer management, estimates, invoices, recurring billing and getting paid.

Last reviewed: September 5, 2026

What SoloBizKit includes

The public toolkit includes business calculators, invoice helpers, PDF tools, QR-code generators and other utilities designed for quick one-off tasks. These core public tools are intended to remain usable without a SoloBizKit account.

SoloBizKit Pro is the private business workspace. It includes a simple CRM, customer history, estimates, invoices, recurring invoice profiles, reusable products and services, reminders, customer portals, attachments, business settings and payment-related workflows.

CRM and customer records

Pro can store customer names, companies, contact details, CRM status, notes, follow-ups and activity history. These records are part of your private workspace and are different from the public browser-only tools.

Estimates and invoicing

Pro supports business defaults, customer-linked estimates and invoices, line items, tax rates, due dates, payment terms, notes and document status. Estimates can be converted into invoices, and invoices can be marked paid or tracked through payment workflows where available.

SoloBizKit does not determine which VAT, sales-tax, invoice numbering, record-retention or legal requirements apply to you. Users remain responsible for the accuracy and compliance of documents they issue.

Recurring billing, reminders and customer portals

Pro includes recurring invoice profiles and reminder workflows so repeat billing can be generated more consistently. Customer portal links can be used to let a customer view relevant estimates or invoices and complete supported actions. Portal links should be treated like private access links and shared only with the intended recipient.

Payments and subscriptions

SoloBizKit Pro is offered as a paid subscription after the advertised trial period. Subscription checkout and billing are handled through Stripe. Where invoice payment links or payment status features are available, payment processing is provided through the connected payment workflow rather than SoloBizKit storing raw card details itself.

Data and privacy

Public tools and Pro do not all process data in the same way. Many free tools run locally in the browser, while signed-in Pro records are stored in the cloud so they can persist across sessions. See the Privacy Policy for the current data categories and service-provider details, and the Security & Data Processing page for access-control and Pro architecture details.

Calculation methodology

Business calculators use deterministic formulas rather than generative AI estimates. See the Methodology page for formulas, assumptions and testing notes.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a broken workflow, inaccurate explanation or privacy issue, use the contact page. We prefer specific, reproducible reports and clear limitations over pretending every workflow fits every business.
