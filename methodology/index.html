Calculator methodology and testing

SoloBizKit shows the core math, defines the assumptions and tests representative cases so you can decide whether a calculator fits your situation.

Formulas documentedEdge cases testedNo hidden AI estimatesCorrections welcomed

Our calculation standard

General business calculators use deterministic formulas. The same inputs produce the same outputs, and calculator results do not come from a generative AI model. Input validation prevents common invalid states such as division by zero, a non-positive loan term or a selling price that cannot cover variable cost.

Scope: These are planning tools. They do not replace a lender's schedule, audited accounts, payroll software, tax calculations or advice from a qualified professional.

Formula reference

Profit margin and markup

Profit = selling price − cost
Margin = profit ÷ selling price × 100
Markup = profit ÷ cost × 100

Break-even

Contribution per unit = selling price − variable cost
Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit, rounded up

Return on investment

Net profit = total return − investment cost
ROI = net profit ÷ investment cost × 100

Annualized ROI uses compound annual growth: (total return ÷ investment cost)12 ÷ months − 1.

Fixed-rate business loan

The loan calculator uses the standard amortizing-payment formula. It simulates the remaining balance month by month when an extra payment is entered. The result assumes a fixed rate and monthly payment frequency.

Cash flow and runway

Monthly net cash flow = monthly cash revenue − monthly cash expenses
Closing cash = opening cash + monthly net cash flow × months
Runway = opening cash ÷ monthly cash deficit

Freelance hourly rate

The rate model divides the annual income target, business costs and buffer by the estimated annual billable hours. Taxes and local employment rules are not inferred automatically.

Automated checks

Core ROI, loan and cash-flow functions have automated tests for standard cases and edge cases. The release audit also checks every indexable route for one H1, a unique title and description, the correct canonical URL, valid JSON-LD, local asset availability, sitemap inclusion and at least one internal link from another page.

Privacy boundary

Business-calculator inputs are processed in your browser. SoloBizKit does not intentionally send the numbers you enter as analytics event parameters. Optional analytics records broad actions, such as opening or completing a tool, only after consent.

Updates and corrections

Material formula changes should update the reviewed date on the affected tool and this methodology page. If you find a calculation error, send the tool name, inputs, expected result and a reproducible explanation to support@solobizkit.it.com or use the contact page.
