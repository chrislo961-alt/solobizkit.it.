How to use this calculator

Use one platform, campaign group or comparable reporting scope at a time. Different platforms define views, impressions and clicks differently, so confirm the reporting definitions before making a direct channel comparison.

The formula

CPM = spend ÷ impressions × 1,000. CPC = spend ÷ clicks. Click-through rate = clicks ÷ impressions × 100.

Worked example

With $1,200 in spend, 350,000 impressions and 2,800 clicks, CPM is $3.43, CPC is $0.43 and click-through rate is 0.8%.

What to include

  • Paid spend after platform adjustments
  • Delivered impressions from the matching dates
  • Link clicks when the goal is website traffic
  • A consistent campaign objective and audience scope

How to interpret the result

CPM measures the price of visibility, not business value. A higher CPM can still be worthwhile if the audience converts better. Use CPC, conversion rate, CAC and profit to connect reach with outcomes.

Keep definitions consistent when comparing periods. Results depend entirely on the values and assumptions entered.

Frequently asked questions

Does organic reach belong in paid CPM?

No. Paid CPM should use paid spend and the impressions attributed to that paid campaign.

Should I use all clicks or link clicks?

Use link clicks when evaluating website traffic. All-click metrics may include profile, reaction or expansion clicks.

Can I compare CPM across social platforms?

Yes, but only as a starting point. Audience quality, placement and impression definitions may differ.
