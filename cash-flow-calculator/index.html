Calculate monthly business cash flow

Positive profit does not always mean positive cash flow. Customers may pay after a sale, inventory may be purchased before it is sold, and loan repayments or equipment purchases can move cash without matching the timing of reported profit.

Monthly net cash flow = cash received − cash paid

Estimate cash runway

When expenses are higher than revenue, runway estimates how long the opening cash balance can cover the repeated monthly deficit. It is a warning indicator, not a promise: real cash timing, minimum balances and unexpected costs can shorten the usable runway.

Make the forecast more realistic

Run a base case, a cautious case with lower revenue and a stress case with higher costs. For seasonal businesses, calculate each month separately rather than using one repeated monthly average.

Cash flow calculator FAQ

Should I enter invoiced revenue or collected cash?

Use cash you expect to receive during the month. An invoice that will be paid later belongs in the later cash period.

What happens when monthly net cash is positive?

The projected balance increases and a depletion runway is not applicable. Positive cash flow can still be interrupted by one-off payments not included in the model.
