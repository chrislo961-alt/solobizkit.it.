JPG to PDF FAQ

Can I combine many JPG files into one PDF?

Yes. Add multiple JPG or PNG files and arrange them in the page order you want before creating the PDF.

Can I change the page order?

Yes. Use the move-up and move-down controls beside each image before creating the document.

Can I convert phone photos to PDF?

Yes, as long as the images are JPG or PNG files and fit within the current browser limits.

Can I convert the PDF back to JPG later?
