About this calculator

Calculate gross profit and gross margin from revenue and cost. Use it as a quick planning tool, then adjust assumptions in the full SoloBizKit calculator when you need more detail.

Why the inputs matter

Small changes in costs, billable utilization, desired income or selling price can materially change the result. Use realistic assumptions and review them as your business changes.

Gross profit vs net profit

Gross profit subtracts direct cost of goods or services from revenue. Net profit also reflects operating expenses, interest, tax and other costs, so it is normally lower than gross profit.
