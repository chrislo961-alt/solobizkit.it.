Create freelance invoices for client work

Freelancers, consultants and independent professionals often need a simple way to bill for project work, hourly services, retainers or other client engagements. A clear invoice helps both you and the client understand what was delivered, what is owed and when payment is due.

What should a freelancer invoice include?

  • Your business or freelancer name and relevant contact details.
  • The client's name, company and billing information.
  • A clear invoice number, issue date and due date.
  • A description of the project, service or billable work.
  • Hours, quantity, rate and line-item totals where relevant.
  • Applicable tax, discounts and the final amount due.
  • Payment terms, payment instructions and notes.

Hourly freelance invoices and project invoices

If you bill by the hour, use the quantity field for billable hours and the rate field for your hourly rate. For fixed-price projects, use quantity 1 and enter the project fee as the rate. For several services or milestones, open the full invoice generator and add multiple line items.

Invoice numbers for freelancers

Use a consistent invoice-number system so you can identify and track invoices later. A format such as INV-2026-0001 is easy to read. The free Invoice Number Generator can help create a repeatable format.

Save a freelance invoice as PDF

The full SoloBizKit Invoice Generator includes client details, dates, multiple line items, tax, discounts, logo, notes and a live preview. When the invoice is finished, use Print / Save PDF and choose your browser or operating system PDF option.

Freelance invoice requirements vary

Invoice, tax, VAT, sales-tax and numbering requirements vary by country, business type and tax status. These tools help build and organize an invoice, but they do not determine which legal or tax fields apply to your situation.

Freelancer invoice FAQ

Can I make a freelance invoice for free?

Yes. The current SoloBizKit invoice tools are free to use and do not require a SoloBizKit account.

What should I write as the invoice description?

Use a description that clearly identifies the work being billed, such as the project name, service delivered, time period or milestone.

Should freelancers charge by hour or project on the invoice?

Use the billing method you agreed with the client. Hourly work can show billable hours and rate, while fixed-fee projects can use one or more project line items.

Can I add multiple services to one invoice?

Yes. Open the full invoice generator to add multiple line items with separate descriptions, quantities and rates.
