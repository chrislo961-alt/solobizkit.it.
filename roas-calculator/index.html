How to use the ROAS Calculator

ROAS measures revenue efficiency, not total profitability. Product cost, agency fees, fulfillment, discounts and refunds can make a campaign unprofitable even when ROAS looks strong. Compare the result with your contribution margin and attribution method before scaling spend.

The formula

ROAS = revenue attributed to ads ÷ advertising spend. ROAS percentage equals that ratio multiplied by 100.

Example

A campaign that attributes $7,500 in revenue to $1,500 in ad spend has a 5.00× ROAS, or 500% return on ad spend.

Questions people ask

What does a 4× ROAS mean?

It means the campaign generated four units of attributed revenue for every one unit spent on advertising.

Is ROAS the same as ROI?

No. ROAS compares ad revenue with ad spend, while ROI normally compares profit with the full investment cost.

What is a good ROAS?

It depends on gross margin, operating costs, attribution and growth goals. A profitable threshold is specific to the business.
